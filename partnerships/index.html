Mindwire + Predictive Index™

Learn How You are Hardwired for Success

MindWire is a Workforce Analytics firm with extensive real world leadership expertise and the best science, tools, and data on the planet. ATHENA International is proud to partner with MindWire® to offer priceless behavioral assessments via The Predictive Index™ to ATHENA Leadership Program participants.

PowerLink™

Accelerating Women Led & Owned Businesses

PowerLink’s flagship advisory board program matches entrepreneurs and business owners with a customized team of volunteer advisors to take their company to the next level by providing a new or fresh perspective on strategy, profitability, operations, capital, sales, and other issues critical to success. Born from this partnership, ATHENA PowerLink brings this program exclusively to women led and owned businesses in our Award Community Partner communities.
